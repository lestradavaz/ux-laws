# UX audit

Use when a persistent audit is requested. Remove irrelevant prompts and populate from actual evidence.

## Boundary and evidence

Record audited journeys, roles, platforms/modalities, environment, and available artifacts. Identify excluded or inaccessible areas and evidence limits.

## Findings

For each finding record severity, location/trigger, evidence, user consequence, relevant reasoning/source, proposed remedy, and verification. Distinguish confidence from severity. Consolidate shared causes.

## Proposed order

Prioritize consequential task failures, access barriers, and recovery risks. Identify dependencies and explain any ordering that differs from severity. Avoid promising implementation effort without examining the code.

## Validation needed

Record which findings can be checked automatically, which require runtime interaction or assistive technology, and which remain research hypotheses. Partial findings do not establish full conformance or product-wide usability.
