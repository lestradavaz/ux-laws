# UX decision record

Use when a persistent decision record is requested. Keep one meaningful decision per record or group decisions that share a tradeoff.

## Decision

State the selected behavior and the affected task.

## Evidence and constraints

Record observed problem, user consequence, expertise/environment, existing conventions, and technical or domain constraints. Distinguish observations, supplied research, and assumptions.

## Reasoning and alternatives

Explain relevant UX principles and applicable requirements. Describe the tradeoff and alternatives only where they materially affect the result. Link authoritative sources for normative or platform-specific claims.

## Verification and consequences

Record checks performed, results, known limitations, and further evidence needed. Include any compatibility or migration consequence actually introduced by the decision.

## Revisit condition

State which new evidence, product change, or failure would justify reconsidering this decision.
