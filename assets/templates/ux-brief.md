# UX brief

Use this template only when a persistent brief is requested. Replace prompts with project facts, supported hypotheses, or explicit unknowns; remove irrelevant sections.

## Outcome and boundary

Describe the user task, requested result, affected journey, and implementation boundary. Identify necessary dependencies separately from optional expansion.

## Users and conditions

Record roles, expertise, frequency, environment, platform, input modalities, and relevant access needs. Identify which facts come from research, product requirements, observation, or assumptions.

## Journey and constraints

Describe starting state, necessary decisions, success state, consequential failures, and recovery. Record existing conventions, data/security constraints, performance needs, and technical limitations that affect behavior.

## Proposed behavior

Describe structure, actions, feedback, states, labels, focus, and control. Explain relevant principles through concrete decisions. Record meaningful alternatives and the chosen tradeoff.

## Acceptance and open questions

List observable acceptance criteria and intended validation. Assign unresolved questions to research, product decisions, or implementation investigation; do not present them as established facts.
